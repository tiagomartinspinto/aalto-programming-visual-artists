Programming for Visual Artists
Session 02 - Color House

You have been given a working sketch where a click changes two colors.
Your task is to find the two variables that store those colors and see
how clicking changes them.

---

STEP 1 - Run It

Run the sketch (or open it in the browser). Click inside the canvas a
few times and watch the house and the sky change color.

---

STEP 2 - Find the Variables

Find houseColor and skyColor near the top of the file. A variable is a
named place where you keep a value so you can read or change it later.
Right now they hold a pink house and a pale blue sky.

---

STEP 3 - Read mousePressed()

mousePressed() runs once, the moment you click. It does not draw
anything itself - it only gives houseColor and skyColor new values.
draw() reads those values on the very next frame, so the shapes update
without draw() ever needing to know a click happened.

---

STEP 4 - Change a Range

Change one of the random() ranges, for example random(150, 255) to
random(0, 100), and click again. Notice what part of the color that
range controls.

---

STEP 5 - Make One Visual Choice

Pick one value in the sketch and set it deliberately instead of
randomly - for example, always make the sky a color you choose. Click
to confirm the house still changes while your sky stays fixed.

---

Nothing about rect() or triangle() changes when you click - only the
two values they read do. That is what a variable is for: a name you
can point at a different value without rewriting the drawing
underneath it.
